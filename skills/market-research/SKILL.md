# Market Research

Research before recommending copy.

## Workflow

1. Define the user decision the page or product must help with.
2. Search current market language, competing products, public-sector guidance, user questions, and relevant community discussions.
3. Separate:
   - verified market facts,
   - competitor claims,
   - user language,
   - hypotheses.
4. Identify the audience's real problem, not the category's marketing vocabulary.
5. Produce concise implications for positioning, content, trust, and conversion.

## ThiefDroppers rules

- Treat ThiefDroppers as global from day one.
- Use jurisdiction-specific material only as a local layer, never as the platform identity.
- Prefer primary sources, regulators, major platforms, credible research, and recent public data.
- For community sentiment, use original Reddit discussions when useful and label anecdotal evidence accordingly.
- Never invent market size, adoption, loss figures, partnerships, user counts, or product capabilities.
- Do not frame the product as antivirus, surveillance, a public suspect database, or an accusation engine.
- Investigate user intent around jobs, recruiters, businesses, messages, websites, payments, coercion, identity misuse, and recovery.

## Output

Return:
- audience/problem,
- market language,
- competitors/alternatives,
- trust gaps,
- SEO/content implications,
- recommended positioning.

Keep evidence and interpretation separate.
